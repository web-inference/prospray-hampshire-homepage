Ask us a question
How much does UPVC spray painting cost?

A single window starts from £150 and a standard UPVC door from £250. Full house jobs range from £900 to £1,800, which is up to 75% cheaper than replacing.

Is spraying cheaper than replacing?

Yes. On average, spraying costs around a quarter of the price of replacement. Replacing windows can cost £400 to £600 each, while spraying starts from £150.

Can you repair a bumper instead of replacing it?

In most cases, yes. We repair scuffs, scratches and dents, then spray to match your paint colour. Send us photos and we will give you a fixed price.

Do you repair chips and scratches before spraying?

Yes. Preparation is part of every job. We fix scratches, stains and cracks first so the finish looks flawless.

Can you spray a whole house or just single items?

We do both. The more areas you spray in one visit, the better the value per item.

How long will the finish last?

We use durable, high-quality coatings designed to last for years, and we back our work with a 10-year guarantee.

Do you work for businesses?

Yes. We spray shopfronts, offices, units and cladding with minimal downtime. Contact us for a custom quote.
